The Difference

Finishing that holds up at arm's length.

Most cabinetry looks fine from across the room. Ours is built to be looked at closely — where the grain meets the edge, where the door meets the frame, where the LED catches the shadow line.

It's a quieter form of craftsmanship. The kind that makes a home feel considered rather than decorated.
